An article in Bengali, published in *Gangeya Janapad* (গাঙ্গেয় জনপদ), on the origin and development of the Natha religious tradition in Bengal. Its abstract, in Bengali, places the subject in the Ganga–Brahmaputra region of Bengal and argues that the tradition originated some centuries earlier than is generally thought (editorial translation).
