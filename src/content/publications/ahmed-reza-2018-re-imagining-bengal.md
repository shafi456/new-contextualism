An edited volume on the architecture, built environment and cultural heritage of Bengal. The founder’s contributions include the introduction, ‘Re-Imagining Bengal: Critical thoughts’ (with I. Ahmed), and chapters on post-Gupta settlement and architecture and on a community-involved management plan for Chhoto Katra in Old Dhaka.
