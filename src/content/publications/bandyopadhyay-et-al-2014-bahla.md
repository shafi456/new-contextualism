A documentation and heritage management plan for Harat al-Aqr at Bahla, listed in the founder’s faculty profile as a UNESCO World Heritage Site. According to the founder’s faculty profile it is one of five such plans for traditional oasis settlements in central Oman (Bahla, Birkat al-Mawz, Fanja, Ibri and Izki), produced through research funded by Nottingham Trent University and Oman’s Ministry of Heritage and Culture.
