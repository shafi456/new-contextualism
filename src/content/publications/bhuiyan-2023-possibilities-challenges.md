According to BRAC University’s report of the workshop, the lecture presented findings on the perceptions and experiences of those already acquainted with the approach, critically analysed its ten key parameters, and identified those with more scope for development. It also discussed how New Contextualism could align with the UN Sustainable Development Goals.
