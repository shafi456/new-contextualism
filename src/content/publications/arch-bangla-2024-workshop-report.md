A report of the workshop ‘Exploring New Contextualism 2.0’, held at the Department of Architecture, BRAC University, on 17 February 2024. It records an introductory session by Mohammad Habib Reza and lectures by Tanjina Khan; S M Kaikobad; Mohammad Zillur Rahman, who ‘elaborated on the methodology of implementing New Contextual theory into architectural and urban projects’; and Md Samiur Rahman Bhuiyan, who addressed the approach’s challenges and prospects and suggested that it ‘could be termed SDG+ (Sustainable Development Goal Plus)’. Hands-on exercises were led by the presenters and ten alumni mentors.
