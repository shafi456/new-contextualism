A report of the June 2023 workshop published by the architecture website Arch-Bangla. It describes New Contextualism as a philosophy coined by Mohammad Habib Reza that addresses the limitations of Modernism and Postmodernism, notes that it emerged at BRAC University, and lists the workshop’s lecture topics and presenters.
