The most explicit statement of the framework appears in the founder’s 2025 essay, which describes ‘a tenfold overlapping analysis’ of context [@reza-2025-deltaic-bangladesh]. The ten dimensions, with the terms the essay uses to characterise each, are listed below.

The purpose of the matrix is practical: it ‘prevents the imposition of predetermined solutions and fosters locally attuned responses’ [@reza-2025-deltaic-bangladesh]. Not every dimension carries equal weight in every project, since the framework’s principles and layers stay constant and ‘only the answers change with place’ [@reza-nitu-2025-people-place-justice]. Published case studies foreground different layers: spiritual, historical, cultural and social conditions in one thesis; ecological, social, political and urban conditions in another [@reza-nitu-2025-people-place-justice].

The formulation has varied over time. The 2023 essay lists the same ten facets without definitions [@reza-2023-new-contextualism-path]. The September 2025 essay names the ten layers among its principles, but its ‘10-layer lens’ lists a spiritual layer in place of the political and calls the scientific layer ‘Scientific/Technological’ [@reza-nitu-2025-people-place-justice]. This platform follows the April 2025 formulation and uses the name ‘Scientific and Technological’ for the sixth dimension, pending confirmation of the definitive version by the founder.
