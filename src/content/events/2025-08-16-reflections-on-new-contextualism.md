The published announcement describes an online symposium bringing together architects, educators and researchers to engage with New Contextualism, ‘a thoughtful architectural philosophy crafted by Dr. Mohammad Habib Reza’ [@contextbd-2025-symposium-listing].

Speakers were to reflect on context-sensitive design across heritage sites, urban landscapes and underserved communities, and on how architectural practice and pedagogy can harmonise innovation with cultural and ecological continuity [@contextbd-2025-symposium-listing]. Participants’ roles are listed as they appeared in the announcement.

The organisers posted updates through the [New Contextual Group on Facebook](https://www.facebook.com/share/g/1LFksgsXDL/).
