According to BRAC University’s report, the lecture series was given by academics and architects who had contributed to the development of New Contextualism. It covered the historical development of the approach, its theoretical framework, case studies and its interdisciplinary nature.

In the hands-on exercise that followed, participants worked in groups to apply its principles to a design challenge, considering the historic, cultural, social and environmental aspects of context [@bracu-2023-workshop-report].
