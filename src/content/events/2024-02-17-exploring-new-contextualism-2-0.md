The published announcement describes a workshop combining lectures, open to all, with hands-on exercises and discussions on: the principles of New Contextualism; the impact of context on design decisions; case studies of New Contextual projects; global practices adopting the approach; possibilities and challenges in contemporary design; and the approach’s potential and limitations [@contextbd-2024-workshop-listing].

Ten alumni of the Department of Architecture were announced as mentors for the hands-on exercises [@contextbd-2024-workshop-listing].
