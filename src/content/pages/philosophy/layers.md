The most explicit statement of the layers appears in the founder’s April 2025 essay, which describes ‘a tenfold overlapping analysis’ of context and characterises each dimension in a few words [@reza-2025-deltaic-bangladesh]. The purpose of the analysis is practical: it ‘prevents the imposition of predetermined solutions and fosters locally attuned responses’ [@reza-2025-deltaic-bangladesh].

<!-- module: layer-table -->

> [!NOTE] Names, terms and order
> The April 2025 essay calls them dimensions; the September 2025 essay calls them layers [@reza-2025-deltaic-bangladesh; @reza-nitu-2025-people-place-justice]. This platform uses ‘layers’. Its ‘10-layer lens’ lists a spiritual layer in place of the political, and calls the scientific layer ‘Scientific/Technological’ [@reza-nitu-2025-people-place-justice]. The layers are listed here in the order of the platform’s structure; the founder’s essays list them in another order, beginning with the social, economic and political [@reza-2025-deltaic-bangladesh]. The definitive formulation awaits confirmation by the founder.

## Social

Who a place is for: its communities, people and ways of life. In the founder’s terms, community structures, demographics and lifestyles [@reza-2025-deltaic-bangladesh]. [The social layer](/philosophy/layers/social/)

## Cultural

How a place carries meaning. In the founder’s terms, traditions, beliefs, aesthetics and rituals [@reza-2025-deltaic-bangladesh]. [The cultural layer](/philosophy/layers/cultural/)

## Historical

What a place remembers. In the founder’s terms, layers of development, memory and significance [@reza-2025-deltaic-bangladesh]. [The historical layer](/philosophy/layers/historical/)

## Political

Who decides: the governance and rules that shape a place. In the founder’s terms, governance, patronage and regulations [@reza-2025-deltaic-bangladesh]. [The political layer](/philosophy/layers/political/)

## Economic

How a place sustains life. In the founder’s terms, livelihoods, industries and affordability [@reza-2025-deltaic-bangladesh]. [The economic layer](/philosophy/layers/economic/)

## Ecological

What a place depends on. In the founder’s terms, climate, topography, biodiversity and resources [@reza-2025-deltaic-bangladesh]. [The ecological layer](/philosophy/layers/ecological/)

## Geographical

Where a place sits. In the founder’s terms, location, connectivity and regional influences [@reza-2025-deltaic-bangladesh]. [The geographical layer](/philosophy/layers/geographical/)

## Scientific

What can be known and built. In the founder’s terms, material properties and technological possibilities [@reza-2025-deltaic-bangladesh]. [The scientific layer](/philosophy/layers/scientific/)

## Urban

How places are organised together. In the founder’s terms, density, infrastructure and public space [@reza-2025-deltaic-bangladesh]. [The urban layer](/philosophy/layers/urban/)

## Architectural

What has been built before. In the founder’s terms, typologies, styles and precedents [@reza-2025-deltaic-bangladesh]. [The architectural layer](/philosophy/layers/architectural/)
