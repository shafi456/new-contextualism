New Contextualism is an approach to architecture and urban design originated by Mohammad Habib Reza, an architect, architectural historian and Associate Professor at the School of Architecture and Design, BRAC University, Dhaka [@reza-2025-deltaic-bangladesh]. In his account it ‘emerged not as a prescriptive style, but as a critical framework’ [@reza-2025-deltaic-bangladesh].

It is a philosophy in development, not a settled doctrine with a single definition. The pages of this section trace how it has been articulated in published texts, and where those texts differ: its definition and origins, its principles, the ten layers through which it reads a place, its position in relation to other theories, and the questions it has yet to settle.
