As a philosophy still in development, New Contextualism invites critical examination, and its own public events have made room for it. The 2023 workshop included a lecture on the possibilities and challenges of the approach, which ‘critically analysed the ten key parameters of the approach and marked which has more scope to work on’ [@bracu-2023-workshop-report; @bhuiyan-2023-possibilities-challenges]. The 2024 workshop programme announced discussion of ‘New Contextualism’s potential and limitations’ [@contextbd-2024-workshop-listing]. The questions below are offered in that spirit, as editorial commentary rather than settled positions. Responses can be proposed through [Community Discussions and Critical Responses](/community/discussions/).

## Weighting the layers

The published texts state that ‘not every layer carries equal weight each time’ [@reza-nitu-2025-people-place-justice], but they do not yet set out how weights are determined, or by whom. A transparent method for weighting layers, and for resolving conflicts between them, would strengthen the framework’s claim to be ‘replicable’ [@reza-nitu-2025-people-place-justice]. See [Evaluation and Trade-offs](/methodologies/evaluation/).

## Prediction and its limits

The approach places considerable trust in prediction, including big data and AI. Its texts qualify this: such tools are to be ‘used responsibly’ [@reza-nitu-2025-people-place-justice], and their accuracy ‘is contingent upon the depth of our understanding of historical precedents and current trends’ [@reza-2025-deltaic-bangladesh]. Questions of data access, bias and accountability, particularly in the Global South, remain open.

## Stability of the framework

The layers and principles have been formulated in different ways across publications, and the names of several related frameworks in this platform’s structure have not yet been defined in print (see [Framework Family and Related Concepts](/philosophy/framework-family/)). A definitive, citable statement approved by the originator would help others apply and critique the framework consistently.

## Evidence from practice

The documented applications are, so far, academic and conceptual proposals, almost all from studios and theses at BRAC University. Built work, post-occupancy evaluation and comparative studies would allow the approach’s claims about equity, resilience and belonging to be tested. See [Projects](/projects/).

## Relation to earlier theory

New Contextualism presents itself as process-based rather than style-based, drawing on Modernism, Postmodernism and critical regionalism ‘in a more nurtured and advanced manner’ [@reza-nitu-2025-people-place-justice]. Careful comparison with that literature is a promising line of research; see [Relationship to Other Theories](/philosophy/related-theories/).

## Independent responses

When this page was last updated, the editors had not found published responses to the approach by authors outside the circle of its proponents. The critical discussion documented so far has taken place at its own workshops [@bracu-2023-workshop-report; @arch-bangla-2024-workshop-report]. Independent reviews and critiques will be added to the archive as they appear.

## Future directions

The founder’s writing anticipates further development through New Contextualism 2.0 [@reza-2025-deltaic-bangladesh], and the 2023 essay was presented as a summary of a forthcoming article for a book dedicated to the philosophy [@reza-2023-new-contextualism-path].
