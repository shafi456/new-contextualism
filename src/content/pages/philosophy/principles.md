The founder presents the principles of New Contextualism as ‘not rigid dogmas, but guiding principles for a more thoughtful and responsible design process’ [@reza-2025-deltaic-bangladesh]. They have been stated several times, with different emphases, and no single formulation has yet been approved as definitive.

## Five guiding principles

The most recent formulation, in the essay of September 2025, sets out five principles under the heading ‘Designing with Time, Data, and Justice’ [@reza-nitu-2025-people-place-justice]:

1. **Forward-thinking design.** ‘Architecture and urban design is for the future, not the present, because by the time a building opens, the present has already passed.’
2. **Informed by prediction.** ‘Big data and AI provide tools to model scenarios, forecast stresses, and anticipate needs.’ Used responsibly, they allow design to remain relevant over time.
3. **Learning from the past.** ‘Every site carries historical and cultural layers that shape identity.’
4. **A broad sense of context.** ‘Context spans more than form or style.’ It operates across scales and across ten overlapping layers.
5. **Equity and justice.** ‘Design must protect the “right to the city,” ensuring that no project strips away people’s access to space, belonging, or shared resources.’

## Propositions behind the principles

Read together, the texts rest on a small number of propositions, summarised here by the editors:

- **Context is wider than the site.** It ‘transcends the immediate physical site’ and ‘encompasses a complex, interwoven matrix of factors’ [@reza-2025-deltaic-bangladesh].
- **The past is the basis for anticipating the future.** History ‘is not an optional academic pursuit for the architect; it is fundamental’, and ‘the past provides the essential data set’ on which prediction can be built [@reza-2025-deltaic-bangladesh].
- **Design should be adaptable.** Buildings and settlements are to be designed ‘not as static objects, but as adaptable systems’ [@reza-2025-deltaic-bangladesh].
- **The universal and the particular can be reconciled.** Global knowledge is embraced, but with ‘its critical adaptation to the specificities of the local’ [@reza-2025-deltaic-bangladesh].
- **Justice is part of design.** The approach places ‘equity and justice’ at its centre [@reza-2023-new-contextualism-path; @reza-2025-deltaic-bangladesh].
