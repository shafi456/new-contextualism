## Foundational Writings

The records of New Contextualism itself: the founder’s essays and articles on the approach, the lectures and reports of its workshops, and writing by its co-authors. [Show only these records](/research/archive/?collection=new-contextualism).

## Related Scholarship

The founder’s wider scholarship, on the architecture, settlements, heritage and civilisational history of Bengal, from which the approach grew. [Show only these records](/research/archive/?collection=founder). See also [Civilizational and Historical Research](/research/civilizational-history/).

## Critical and Comparative Literature

Works cited in the texts of New Contextualism, followed by related literature selected by the editors, including other uses of the phrase ‘new contextualism’ and responses to the founder’s writing. Inclusion here does not imply that an author is associated with the approach.
