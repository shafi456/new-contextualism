The archive gathers the essays, articles, lectures and reports in which New Contextualism has been set out and discussed, together with the founder’s research on the architecture and history of Bengal. Each record has a page of its own, with a summary, its full citation in Chicago, APA, BibTeX and RIS formats, and links to the original where it is available online.

The records come from sources that can be read and checked. Where a book or guide has been announced but not published, the archive says so rather than listing it.
