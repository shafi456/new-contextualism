The works below are those cited in the texts of New Contextualism, with a few related works chosen by the editors. They are arranged by the themes discussed under [Relationship to Other Theories](/philosophy/related-theories/). Full references are listed at the end of the page.

## The texts of New Contextualism

Begin with the three essays in which the approach is set out: the founder’s essays of 2023 and April 2025 [@reza-2023-new-contextualism-path; @reza-2025-deltaic-bangladesh], and the essay of September 2025 by the founder and Upama Das Nitu [@reza-nitu-2025-people-place-justice].

## Contextualism and its critics

- Colin Rowe and Fred Koetter on the city as collage, the classic statement of contextualist urban design [@rowe-koetter-1978-collage-city].
- Robert Venturi on complexity and contradiction, and Charles Jencks on the language of Post-Modern architecture, both named by the founder [@venturi-1966-complexity; @jencks-1977-language].
- Esin Komez Daglioglu’s survey of the ‘context debate’ [@komez-daglioglu-2015-context-debate].

## Critical regionalism

- Kenneth Frampton’s six points for an architecture of resistance, cited in the September 2025 essay [@frampton-1983-critical-regionalism].

## Place and the city

- Kevin Lynch on the image of the city, and Edward Relph on place and placelessness, both cited in the September 2025 essay [@lynch-1960-image-of-the-city; @relph-1976-place-and-placelessness].
- Christian Norberg-Schulz on the spirit of place [@norberg-schulz-1980-genius-loci].
- Jane Jacobs and Jan Gehl on the life of cities and cities for people, both named by the founder [@jacobs-1961-death-and-life; @gehl-2010-cities-for-people].
- Christopher Alexander and colleagues on a pattern language [@alexander-et-al-1977-pattern-language].

## Justice and sustainability

- Henri Lefebvre on the right to the city [@lefebvre-1968-droit-a-la-ville].
- The United Nations 2030 Agenda and its Sustainable Development Goals, to which New Contextualism 2.0 aligns itself [@united-nations-2015-agenda-2030].
