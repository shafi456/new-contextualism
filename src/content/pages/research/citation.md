## How to Cite New Contextualism

Cite the founder’s published texts for the ideas of New Contextualism, not this platform. The three principal statements so far are:

- Reza, Mohammad Habib. 2023. ‘New contextualism: A path to a sustainable and equitable future.’ *The Business Standard*, 19 December 2023 [@reza-2023-new-contextualism-path].
- Reza, Mohammad Habib. 2025. ‘New Contextualism: An architectural philosophy for deltaic Bangladesh.’ *The Daily Star*, 23 April 2025 [@reza-2025-deltaic-bangladesh].
- Reza, Mohammad Habib, and Upama Das Nitu. 2025. ‘New Contextualism: Architecture and Urbanism for People, Place, and Justice.’ *Context BD*, 16 September 2025 [@reza-nitu-2025-people-place-justice].

Each record page gives the citation in Chicago author–date, APA, BibTeX and RIS formats, ready to copy. Cite this platform only for its editorial material, such as summaries, diagrams and the relations proposed between layers, which are not statements by the founder.

## DOI and Permanent Links

Most records of New Contextualism are newspaper and magazine essays without a DOI. Where a record has one, it is given on the record’s page and listed above.

Every record, project and layer on this platform has a permanent address, which is not changed once published:

- records of the archive: /research/*record*/
- projects: /projects/*project*/
- the ten layers: /philosophy/layers/*layer*/

Addresses from the first edition of the platform redirect to their new places.
