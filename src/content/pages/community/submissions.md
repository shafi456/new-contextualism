The archive welcomes proposals for research, case studies, critical responses and documentation of projects that apply, extend or question the approach. This page describes how submissions work. The arrangements are proposed, pending the founder’s approval.

## What can be submitted

- **Documentation of projects**: theses, studio work, competition entries and built work that apply the approach, with the permission of their authors.
- **Research**: articles, papers and reports on New Contextualism.
- **Critical responses**: reviews, critiques and comparisons, including those that disagree with the approach.
- **Corrections**: errors of fact, citation or attribution anywhere on the platform.

## What to include

- The title, the names of everyone credited, and the year.
- Where the work has been published or presented, with a link if there is one. The platform documents work from sources that readers can check.
- For images and drawings, confirmation that you hold the rights to them, and the credit to be shown with each.
- A short summary, in your own words, of how the work relates to New Contextualism.

## How to submit

<!-- module: contact -->

Submissions are reviewed by the editors as described under [Contributions and Review Process](/governance/review-process/). Material is published with full attribution and its sources, and only with its authors’ permission. This platform is not a membership organisation, and submitting work does not make anyone a member of one.
