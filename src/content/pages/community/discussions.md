The critical discussion of New Contextualism documented so far has taken place at its own events, in lectures given by colleagues of the founder.

## At the workshops

The 2023 workshop included a lecture on the possibilities and challenges of the approach, which ‘critically analysed the ten key parameters of the approach and marked which has more scope to work on’ [@bracu-2023-workshop-report].

<!-- module: records topic="critique" empty="No lectures on the critique of the approach have yet been added." -->

The programme of the 2024 workshop announced discussion of ‘New Contextualism’s potential and limitations’ [@contextbd-2024-workshop-listing]. At that workshop, Md Samiur Rahman Bhuiyan addressed the challenges and prospects of the approach and its alignment with the Sustainable Development Goals, suggesting that it ‘could be termed SDG+ (Sustainable Development Goal Plus)’ [@arch-bangla-2024-workshop-report].

## Independent responses

When this page was last updated, the editors had not found published reviews or critiques of New Contextualism by authors outside the circle of its proponents. A 2026 column in the *Dhaka Courier* responds to the founder’s historical writing, not to New Contextualism [@latif-2026-rezas-bengal].

Questions that the approach has yet to settle are set out under [Critical Perspectives and Open Questions](/philosophy/critical-perspectives/).

## Adding a response

Published responses, whether in agreement or disagreement, can be proposed for the archive through [Community Submissions](/community/submissions/). The platform does not host comments or a discussion forum; responses are recorded once they have been published elsewhere, so that they can be cited.
