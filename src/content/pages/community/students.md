Most of the documented work on New Contextualism has been made by students, in the studios and thesis supervision through which the approach has been developed at BRAC University [@reza-2023-new-contextualism-path; @reza-nitu-2025-people-place-justice].

## Studios and theses

In the founder’s design studios, students are encouraged ‘to embrace New Contextualism through a tenfold overlapping study of the broader context’ [@reza-2023-new-contextualism-path]. Theses documented on this platform are listed under [Teaching and Studio Projects](/projects/teaching-and-studio/), with further student work published elsewhere.

## Workshops

Both public workshops combined lectures with hands-on exercises. In 2023, participants applied the approach to a design challenge in groups [@bracu-2023-workshop-report]; in 2024, the exercises were led by the presenters and ten alumni mentors [@arch-bangla-2024-workshop-report]. See [Events](/community/events/).

## Competitions

Student entries framed by the approach have been placed in international competitions. An entry on water edges secured second position in a competition organised by the Aditya School of Architecture, Mumbai [@contextbd-2024-value-of-null], and an entry that ‘followed the principles of new contextualism’ was among the winning entries of the Surplus Properties for Housing competition hosted by Dalhousie University and Arch Hive in 2024 [@bracu-express-2024-bridging-the-gap].

## Opportunities

> [!PENDING] None open at present
> No scholarships, internships, studios or calls for students are currently offered through this platform. Opportunities will be published here, with their terms and deadlines, when they are announced.

The [proposed governance structure](/governance/#proposed-governance-structure) includes an NC Student Nexus: an independent, student-led space for research, peer critique and reading groups, organised in regional chapters and hosting annual challenges. It has not yet been formed.

A guide to New Contextualism for students, co-edited by Upama Das Nitu in collaboration with the founder, is listed as in progress [@bracu-nitu-profile].
