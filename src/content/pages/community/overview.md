New Contextualism has developed through design studios, workshops, lectures and symposia, and through exchange between practice and academia. Its public life so far can be traced through two workshops at BRAC University, in 2023 and 2024, and an online symposium in 2025 [@bracu-2023-workshop-report; @contextbd-2024-workshop-listing; @contextbd-2025-symposium-listing].

This platform is intended as a place for that dialogue to continue, across disciplines and between local conditions and wider systems. It is not a membership organisation, and it charges no fees for any form of participation. The ways to take part are set out in the pages of this section; each describes what is in place now and what is still to come.
