The evidence of use documented so far comes from teaching at one university: studios, theses and competition entries by students and teachers of BRAC University. It is listed here as published.

## Institutions and Programmes

New Contextualism has been ‘cultivated at BRAC University, where it has been embraced by students and faculty alike’ [@reza-2023-new-contextualism-path], and the founder has conducted ‘numerous undergraduate thesis studios on this philosophy’ there [@reza-nitu-2025-people-place-justice]. The university’s Department of Architecture hosted the workshops of 2023 and 2024 [@bracu-2023-workshop-report; @contextbd-2024-workshop-listing].

> [!PENDING] Other institutions
> No use of New Contextualism at another institution or in another programme has yet been documented. Institutions that teach or test the approach are invited to propose documentation through [Community Submissions](/community/submissions/).

## Practitioners and Studios

The online symposium of 2025, ‘Reflections on New Contextualism: Voices from Practice and Academia’, included speakers working in architectural practice [@contextbd-2025-symposium-listing]. Their participation is not, by itself, evidence that their practices use the approach.

> [!PENDING] None documented
> No architectural or urban design practice has yet been documented as applying New Contextualism to a built or commissioned project.

## Courses Using New Contextualism

The approach is documented in the founder’s design studios, where students undertake ‘a tenfold overlapping study of the broader context’ [@reza-2023-new-contextualism-path], and in the thesis studio (Studio X) at BRAC University, where several theses describe themselves as founded on it or inspired by it [@arch-bangla-2024-ahsania-mission-children-city; @arch-bangla-2024-mru-settlement]. It is also documented in the fourth-year housing studio ARC402 in 2024, where a group project mentored by the founder was framed by ‘the philosophy of “New Contextualism”’ [@arch-bangla-2025-nexus-cityscape].

No course or syllabus named after New Contextualism has been found.
