The approach has so far been developed within one institution. It has been cultivated in teaching at BRAC University [@reza-2023-new-contextualism-path], whose Department of Architecture hosted its 2023 and 2024 workshops [@bracu-2023-workshop-report; @contextbd-2024-workshop-listing]. The 2025 online symposium was organised under the name of the New Contextual Group [@contextbd-2025-symposium-listing]. Neither is presented here as a partnership.

## Proposing a collaboration

Institutions, teachers and researchers who wish to test the framework in their own contexts, through studios, theses or joint research, are invited to write to the editors. Proposals are handled as described under [Contributions and Review Process](/about/review-process/); the address for correspondence is given under [Contact](/about/contact/).
