> [!NOTE] No donations
> The platform does not ask for or accept donations, and no fund has been set up in its name. If that changes, it will be announced here, with who holds the funds and how they are used.

The most useful forms of support are contributions to the record.

## Share and cite

Cite the founder’s published texts, and link to the records of the archive. See [Citation Tools](/research/citation/).

## Document work

If you have made or supervised work that applies the approach, propose its documentation, with its authors’ permission; see [Community Submissions](/community/submissions/).

## Respond

Published responses, critical or not, help the approach develop. They can be proposed for the archive through [Community Submissions](/community/submissions/).

## Correct the record

Report errors of fact, citation or attribution; see [Corrections and Updates](/governance/corrections/).

## Share images

The projects on this platform are illustrated with abstract drawings until their authors supply photographs and drawings. If you hold the rights to images of a documented project, please write to the editors; see [Contact](/governance/contact/).
