## Publications that have covered the approach

- [Arch-Bangla](https://arch-bangla.com/), which reported the workshops of 2023 and 2024 and has published student projects that draw on the approach.
- [Esempi di Architettura, vol. 13, no. 2](https://www.aracneeditrice.eu/it/pubblicazioni/esempi-di-architettura-olimpia-niglio-rana-p-b-singh-9791221824766.html), the 2026 journal issue with the article on Somapura Mahavihara.
