> The platform does not yet send an email newsletter, and it does not collect email addresses. If a newsletter is started, how to subscribe, and how addresses are handled, will be published here and in the [Privacy and Terms of Use](/governance/privacy-and-terms/).

In the meantime, new records and events can be followed through the platform’s feed. A feed reader checks it for new entries; no sign-up is needed, and nothing is collected about the people who follow it.
