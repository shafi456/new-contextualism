This statement is a draft, to be reviewed by the platform’s administrators before launch.

## No tracking

This website does not use cookies, analytics, advertising or tracking scripts. It does not embed third-party media or social-media widgets, and its typefaces are served from this website rather than from a third-party font service.

## Search and filters

The site search and the archive filters run entirely in your browser. Search terms are not sent to us or to anyone else. Filter choices appear in the address bar so that a filtered view of the archive can be shared.

## Hosting

Like any website, the server that hosts this site may record technical information, such as IP addresses, in its access logs. The hosting provider and the period for which such logs are kept will be stated here once confirmed.

## Correspondence

If you write to the editors, your message and address are used only to reply and to handle your enquiry. Correspondents are not added to mailing lists.

## Editors

The editorial interface is used only by authorised editors. When editor sign-in is provided through GitHub, editors’ accounts are handled by GitHub under its own privacy policy.

## External links

Links to other websites, such as publishers, newspapers and institutional profiles, are governed by those websites’ own policies.
