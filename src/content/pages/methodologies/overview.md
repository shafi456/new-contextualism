In practice, New Contextualism is presented as a process before it is a form. Every project is said to begin with three phases, Read, Plan and Process, with particular weight given to the first: ‘Before form or function, it is necessary to understand the context’ [@reza-nitu-2025-people-place-justice]. The ten contextual layers give that reading its structure.

This section sets out the framework and the method as the published texts describe them, and how they bear on architectural design, urbanism and heritage management. It also gathers the analytical tools and resources offered on this platform.

> [!NOTE] What is documented, and what is not
> The published texts describe the framework and the phase of reading in some detail. They say less about planning, design development and evaluation, and the related methodologies named in this platform’s structure have not yet been defined in print. Each page states what the sources say and marks what awaits documentation.
