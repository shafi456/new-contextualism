Most of the documented applications of New Contextualism are architectural design proposals, made in studios and theses at BRAC University. This page draws on them and on the founder’s essays.

## New Contextual Design Methodology (NCDM)

> [!PENDING] Not yet defined in a published source
> The name New Contextual Design Methodology has not been found in a published source. A definition will be added here when the founder publishes or approves one.

The published texts describe a design method without giving it this name: the ‘tenfold overlapping study’ of the founder’s studios [@reza-2023-new-contextualism-path] and the sequence of [Read, Plan and Process](/methodologies/read-plan-process/) [@reza-nitu-2025-people-place-justice]. At the 2024 workshop, Mohammad Zillur Rahman ‘elaborated on the methodology of implementing New Contextual theory into architectural and urban projects, drawing from previous student projects and real-world examples’ [@arch-bangla-2024-workshop-report]; the content of that lecture has not been published.

## Reading Architectural Context

The architectural layer reads what has been built before: typologies, styles and precedents [@reza-2025-deltaic-bangladesh]. The founder’s examples come from the history of Bengal. Early Buddhist monasteries were ‘a sophisticated response to the local climate’ [@reza-2025-deltaic-bangladesh], and the Sultanate mosque, ‘incorporating elements like the curved cornice derived from bamboo construction’, exemplifies the synthesis of universal and local knowledge [@reza-2025-deltaic-bangladesh]. See [the architectural layer](/philosophy/layers/architectural/).

## Context-Informed Design Decisions

Reading informs design without dictating its forms. The approach ‘does not mean blindly mimicking old architectural styles’ [@reza-2023-new-contextualism-path]. The founder’s example is the transitional semi-open space that older buildings used to mitigate a hot, humid climate, which can be reinterpreted in contemporary buildings ‘through elements like sun-shading louvres, jalis and buffers’ [@reza-2023-new-contextualism-path].

In the documented proposals, decisions follow from the layers that were found to matter. In the Panatirtha thesis, ‘courts for culture and for ritual were not divided but interwoven, open to all identities’ [@reza-nitu-2025-people-place-justice]. The thesis on Jamuna Future Park opens a shopping complex ‘to pedestrian networks, green corridors, and social programs’, envisioning it ‘as an inclusive urban platform’ [@reza-nitu-2025-people-place-justice].

## Design Evaluation

> [!PENDING] Awaiting documentation
> No published source sets out criteria or procedures for evaluating designs made with the approach.

The September 2025 essay states the outcomes it expects: ‘When the ten-layer lens works in concert with the five principles, we produce buildings and spaces that are lower-carbon, more inclusive, and genuinely loved’ [@reza-nitu-2025-people-place-justice]. Its three aims (sense of place and identity, sustainability and social engagement) suggest the criteria by which designs might be assessed; that reading is the editors’.
