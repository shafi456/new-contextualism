## Contextual Knowledge and Decision-Making

The published texts say a good deal about how knowledge of context should inform decisions. The September 2025 essay starts from a diagnosis: ‘Too often, planners, urban designers, and even city authorities operate without clear guidance. Thus, briefs remain vague, decisions pile up incoherently, and when trade-offs become difficult, ethics are overlooked’ [@reza-nitu-2025-people-place-justice].

The founder’s 2023 essay describes the knowledge on which better decisions draw: ‘Designers can better comprehend potential future scenarios through data analytics, scenario planning and expert insights’ [@reza-2023-new-contextualism-path]. Such knowledge depends on history. Predictive tools are only as accurate as ‘the depth of our understanding of historical precedents and current trends’ [@reza-2025-deltaic-bangladesh].
