> [!PENDING] Not yet constituted
> No advisory network has yet been formed for this platform. If one is constituted, its members, how they were invited and its terms of reference will be published here.

The [proposed governance structure](/governance/) includes a Practice & Policy Council of architects, urban designers, heritage professionals, planners and policymakers, and an Open Assembly for public debate. Neither has yet been constituted.

The people who have contributed to the public record of New Contextualism, as authors, lecturers and speakers, are listed under [Contributors](/community/contributors/). Inclusion in that list does not make anyone an adviser to the platform.
