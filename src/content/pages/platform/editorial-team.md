## What the editors do

The editors compile and check the platform’s content against its sources, write the summaries marked as editorial, propose the statements marked as proposed, and correct errors. They work to the [Editorial Standards](/about/editorial-standards/). The statement of the philosophy, the founder’s profile and the branding are approved by the founder, not by the editors.

In the [governance of the movement](/governance/), the stewardship of the theory itself, including the evaluation of proposed extensions, rests with the Curatorial Core.
