This statement is a draft, to be reviewed before the platform is formally launched.

## Quoted texts and records

Quotations and the works recorded in the archive remain the copyright of their authors and publishers. Quotations are kept short, attributed and linked to their sources, for the purposes of criticism, research and reference. Readers who wish to reproduce a work should consult its publisher.

## The platform’s own texts and drawings

The editorial texts and the original drawings of this platform are made for it. The terms on which they may be reused have not yet been decided; until they are, please ask the editors before reproducing them, and cite them as described under [Citation Tools](/research/citation/).

## Contributed material

Contributors keep the copyright in the work they submit, and grant the platform permission to publish it with attribution. Photographs and drawings of projects are published only with the permission of their authors, and with a credit.

## Data and typefaces

The base map of the [Project Map](/map/) is made with Natural Earth data, which is in the public domain. The typefaces, Instrument Serif and Inter, are published under the SIL Open Font License and are served from this website.

## Requests

To ask about permissions, or to report material that you believe infringes your rights, write to the editors; see [Contact](/governance/contact/).
