These arrangements are proposed, pending the founder’s approval. What can be submitted, and what to include, is set out under [Community Submissions](/community/submissions/).

## Review

Every proposal is read by the editors. They check that the work can be documented from a source that readers can consult, and that its account of New Contextualism is accurate. They check that its authors agree to its publication, and that images come with permission and a credit. Proposals that concern the statement of the philosophy are referred to the founder. Under the [proposed governance structure](/governance/), proposed extensions of the theory would also be evaluated by a Curatorial Core, once it is constituted.

## Decisions

The editors reply to every proposal. A proposal may be accepted, accepted with changes agreed with its authors, or declined, with the reason given. Work is not declined for disagreeing with the approach: critical responses are part of the record.

## Publication and attribution

Accepted material is published with full attribution, the date of publication on the platform and its sources, and is labelled according to the [Editorial Standards](/governance/editorial-standards/). Contributors keep the rights to their work; see [Intellectual Property and Copyright](/governance/copyright/).

## Changes after publication

Contributors may ask for corrections to, or the withdrawal of, their material at any time. Changes are recorded as described under [Corrections and Updates](/governance/corrections/).
