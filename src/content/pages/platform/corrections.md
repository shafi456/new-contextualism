## Reporting an error

Errors of fact, citation or attribution can be reported to the editors; see [Contact](/about/contact/). Please give the address of the page, the passage concerned and, if possible, the source that shows the error.

## How corrections are made

Errors are corrected promptly. A significant correction, one that changes a statement of fact, a quotation or an attribution, is noted on the page concerned with the date of the correction, and recorded below. Every page shows the date on which it was last updated.
