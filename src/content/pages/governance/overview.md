The governance of New Contextualism as a movement is organised around five bodies: one to steward its theory, one to carry it into teaching, one to test it in practice and policy, one to hold its public debate, and one led by students. Together they are meant to keep the foundations of the framework stable while it is extended, taught, applied and questioned.
