## Intellectual origins

The founder traces New Contextualism to his early work as an architect-planner for the Barisal City Corporation and to his doctoral research on the early Buddhist architecture of Bengal [@reza-2023-new-contextualism-path; @reza-2025-deltaic-bangladesh]. The approach crystallised, in his words, from ‘the practical challenges of contemporary urban planning in Bangladesh, the deep historical insights from our region's architectural past, and a critical engagement with global architectural discourse’ [@reza-2025-deltaic-bangladesh]. The full account is given under [Origins and Evolution](/philosophy/origins-and-evolution/).
