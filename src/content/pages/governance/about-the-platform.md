This website is intended as the digital home of New Contextualism: a place to read its philosophy, consult its framework, follow its applications and trace its published record.

It is organised as an archive as much as a publication. Statements about the approach are linked to their sources, and every record has a persistent address so that it can be cited. Where the record is incomplete, the site says so rather than filling the gap.

The platform is in a pre-publication phase. Its texts have been compiled from published sources and editorial drafts. The statement of the philosophy, the founder’s profile and the platform’s branding are awaiting the founder’s approval.

## Proposed governance structure

A structure of five bodies has been proposed for the governance of New Contextualism as a movement: one to steward its theory, one to carry it into teaching, one to test it in practice and policy, one to hold its public debate, and one led by students. Together they are meant to keep the foundations of the framework stable while it is extended, taught, applied and questioned.

> [!PENDING] A proposal
> None of these bodies has yet been formally constituted, and no members are listed. The structure awaits the founder’s approval. When a body is formed, its members, how they were chosen and its terms of reference will be published here.
