This website is intended as the digital home of New Contextualism: a place to read its philosophy, consult its framework, follow its applications and trace its published record.

It is organised as an archive as much as a publication. Statements about the movement are linked to their sources, and every record has a persistent address so that it can be cited. Where the record is incomplete, the site says so rather than filling the gap.

The platform is in a pre-publication phase. Its texts have been compiled from published sources and editorial drafts. The statement of the philosophy, the founder’s profile and the platform’s branding are awaiting the founder’s approval.
