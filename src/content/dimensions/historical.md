The historical layer reads place as an accumulation of layers of development, memory and significance [@reza-2025-deltaic-bangladesh]. New Contextualism treats history as foundational knowledge rather than an optional pursuit: it illuminates how societies evolved, the successes and failures of earlier interventions, and the long-term consequences of design decisions [@reza-2025-deltaic-bangladesh].

History is also the basis of anticipation. The founder argues that the past provides ‘the essential data set’ on which meaningful prediction can be built [@reza-2025-deltaic-bangladesh]. At the same time the approach rejects ‘a retreat into uncritical nostalgia’ [@reza-2025-deltaic-bangladesh]: the task is to reinterpret the past intelligently, not to mimic old architectural styles [@reza-2023-new-contextualism-path].
