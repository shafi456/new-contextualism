The architectural layer concerns the discipline’s own knowledge: typologies, styles and precedents [@reza-2025-deltaic-bangladesh]. It asks how earlier buildings solved comparable problems, and how their lessons can be carried forward without imitation.

The founder’s 2023 essay gives an example: the transitional semi-open spaces with which earlier buildings mitigated a hot, humid climate can be reinterpreted through sun-shading louvres, jalis and buffers [@reza-2023-new-contextualism-path]. The 2025 essay cites the Sultanate mosque in Bengal, with its curved cornice derived from bamboo construction, as an example of successful synthesis between imported typologies and local building traditions [@reza-2025-deltaic-bangladesh].
