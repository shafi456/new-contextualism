The cultural layer concerns how places carry meaning: traditions, beliefs, aesthetics and rituals [@reza-2025-deltaic-bangladesh]. It resists what New Contextualist writing describes as cultural homogenisation, the ‘anywhere city’ of glass towers, shopping malls and flyovers in which local materials, traditions and narratives are sidelined [@reza-nitu-2025-people-place-justice].

Culture is not treated as a stylistic repertoire to be copied. The approach favours hybridisation, filtering global knowledge ‘through the lens of local climate, materials, craftsmanship, cultural norms, and aesthetic sensibilities’ [@reza-2025-deltaic-bangladesh]. In the Panatirtha thesis a philosophical tradition, Advaita or non-dualism, shaped the spatial form itself [@reza-nitu-2025-people-place-justice].
