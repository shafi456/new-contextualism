The urban layer concerns the collective organisation of settlement: density, infrastructure and public space [@reza-2025-deltaic-bangladesh]. It reads streets, waterfronts, transit and open space as the shared frame within which buildings acquire their meaning.

New Contextualist writing describes cities in which a highway cuts through what was once a neighbourhood and a once-lively waterfront is ‘reduced to sewage and concrete embankments’ [@reza-nitu-2025-people-place-justice]. Its case studies respond at the scale of the city: the Kallyanpur Khal thesis framed a neglected canal as a civic spine, combining flood management with community access within a transit-oriented development [@reza-nitu-2025-people-place-justice].
